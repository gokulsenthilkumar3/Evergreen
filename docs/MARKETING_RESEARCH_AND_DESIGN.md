# EverGreen marketing synthesis

Research and implementation: 1 November 2026. Scope: the public marketing homepage at port 3300, with the six existing design studies retained at `/demo/Theme{Name}/`.

## Direction

**Every thread. One clear story.** Combine a memorable textile object with a practical journey through records, people and discovery. Forest green, warm paper, restrained brass, editorial serif accents and open spacing give the mill story a recognisable identity. The main product experience is `apps/marketing-web/src/UnifiedMarketing.tsx`; the previous implementation remains in `App.tsx` as source reference.

The supplied `apps/marketing-web/Refernce.md` is an inspiration brief, not evidence that its proposed telemetry, savings, integrations or service guarantees exist. The redesign removes unsupported figures and promises from the active homepage and its metadata. It uses no invented customer logos, quotations or market-share numbers.

## People, customers and emotions

Emotional needs below are design hypotheses to validate with users, not national stereotypes. [Better Work training](https://www.betterwork.org/training/) supports attention to learning and workplace communication. Avoid fear-based crisis effects or messages that blame floor workers.

| Audience | Likely concern to validate | Desired feeling | Implemented response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Unclear quantities and outstanding work | Clarity and control | Reviewable sample mass balance and readiness information |
| Accounts | Missing source records and uncertain import mapping | Confidence | Explicit operator-reviewed Tally handoff |
| Floor team | Repeated searching and incomplete shift context | Competence and calm | Short input/output story and a clear next action |
| Buyer | Promises made without confirmed availability | Confidence | Catalogue/stock confirmation guidance without fictional stock |
| Small mill | Adoption effort and training | Approachability | Short guided journey and lightweight controls |
| Integrated manufacturer | Process boundaries and external systems | Informed control | Material stages, scoped readiness and discovery checklist |
| Trading/sourcing company | Partner and order handoffs | Continuity | Buyer perspective and market-specific questions |

## Interaction and evidence boundaries

- All four roles, four handoffs and five market selections update real page content.
- Missing-receipt switch exposes a record gap; it does not claim automatic remediation.
- Calculator: output = input × (1 − waste percentage / 100). It does not predict savings, margins or investment returns.
- Demo brief captures company, business type, selected market, role and workflow locally. Download does not send an enquiry or book a meeting.
- Public contact settings use the existing marketing projection; unavailable settings retain the confirmed default.
- Scout retains the existing server endpoint and opt-in assistant component. No credential enters the frontend.
- Reduced-motion preference disables animation; pause control is also available. No autoplay audio, scroll interception, forced 3D renderer or new animation library.

## Follow-up validation

Interview owners, accounts staff, operators and buyers in each proposed market before treating audience assumptions as established. Validate keyboard use, narrow-screen layout and actual human comprehension. Live international integrations, machine telemetry and local legal requirements need their own verification. Visual browser inspection remains a separate outstanding check when browser automation becomes available.
