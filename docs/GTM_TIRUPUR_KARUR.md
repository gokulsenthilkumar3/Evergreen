## 1. Executive Summary & Market Reality

The Tamil Nadu spinning and knitting corridor processes over **40% of India's spun yarn**. In Tirupur, Karur, and Coimbatore, standard enterprise software sales cycles stall because:
1. **The Status Quo Works "Well Enough":** Mills have run on physical registers (A-Book / B-Book / Day Book) and Tally for 25+ years.
2. **Accountants are the Ultimate Gatekeepers:** Any tool that attempts to replace Tally Prime will be actively sabotaged or blocked by the head accountant.
3. **Software Trust Deficit:** Mill owners have repeatedly paid Coimbatore IT vendors ₹2-5 Lakhs for custom desktop ERPs that broke within 6 months.

### The Winning Vector: "Anti-Fraud Gateway & Tally Feeder"
We do not pitch an ERP. We pitch **TareZero**:

## 5. 7-Day Grace Quota & Commercial Renewal Protocol

To protect cash flow and eliminate the liability of perpetual free usage:
1. **The 7-Day Grace Window:** If an annual/monthly renewal is pending, the system switches to **Active Grace Mode** for 7 calendar days (reduced from 30 days).
2. **Operational Continuity:** Factory gate inward and outward remain 100% functional so trucks are never stranded at the gate.
3. **Escalation Notification:**
   - Day 1-3: Polite amber banner in Tenant UI with 1-click renewal invoice link.
   - Day 4-6: Automated WhatsApp reminder to Mill MD and Provider Admin dashboard alert.
   - Day 7: Quota read-only enforcement if not settled.
4. **1-Minute Reactivation:** As soon as payment UTR is verified in Provider Admin (Port 5174), the tenant's license quota resets in under 60 seconds with zero downtime.
