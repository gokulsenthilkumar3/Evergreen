    Trust and support
    Evidence first, promises second.
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Are there customer stories?
    Not yet on this page. Any story we publish will name the mill, the period and the measured change, and will come with permission. Sample scenarios are labelled as such.
    
        
    Are the numbers on this page real results?
    No. Figures in the simulator and calculator are examples or your own inputs. Verified outcomes come from lot-level records after a pilot.
    
        
    What about compliance and SLAs?
    Compliance claims and service levels are confirmed in writing for your plan. Nothing here is a certification or a guarantee.
    
        
    Does EverGreen connect to Tally?
    The app can export Tally XML. Other connectors are planned profiles, as listed above.
    
        
    What happens to the contact form?
    It prepares a draft for you to copy and send. It does not deliver anything until a delivery endpoint is configured for the build.
    
      
    
      
    Slot reserved
    Verified evidence goes here
    A published story needs a mill name, a baseline period, a measured result and sign-off. Until those exist, this space stays empty on purpose.
    Support commitments, such as hours and response times, are agreed per plan and listed in your order form.
