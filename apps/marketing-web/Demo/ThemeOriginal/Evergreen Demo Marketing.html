Platform

Six workflows, one continuous ledger

Material arrives, becomes yarn, becomes fabric, leaves as a dispatch and returns as money. EverGreen keeps that thread unbroken — every step writes to the same record instead of a separate spreadsheet.

  1. InwardGRN, lots, quality
  2. ProductionOrders, stages, waste
  3. InventoryGodown, batch, value
  4. Job workOut, in, reconciliation
  5. CommerceOrders, dispatch, returns
  6. InvoicesGST, ledger, Tally XML

Inward & lots

Record bales, beams and batches against a lot with quality readings, so downstream stages inherit the same identity.

IllustrativeSample fields shown

Production

Track orders through warping, weaving and finishing with stage-wise output and waste captured as it happens.

IllustrativeStage list is configurable

Inventory

Godown-wise, batch-wise stock with moving value — including what is out on job work, which is where most books drift.

IllustrativeValuation method varies

Job work

Send material out, receive it back, reconcile shortage and wastage against the original challan — with age of every open item.

IllustrativeChallan formats vary by state

Commerce

Sales orders, dispatch and returns connected to the same stock that production wrote to — no parallel order register.

PlannedNot shipped in this build

Invoices

Generate GST invoices from dispatched stock, then export vouchers as Tally XML — the one integration that exists in the app today.

Evidence & FAQ

      
What we can and cannot show you yet

      

        A marketing page that invents customer quotes is worse than no marketing page. Below is
        what is verifiable today, and what is still a plan.
      

    


    

      

        Policy
        
No illustrative testimonials

        

          We do not publish sample case studies, invented savings percentages or placeholder
          customer logos. When a named mill agrees to be referenced — with figures they have
          checked — it will appear here with their approval and the date.
        

        

          Until then, the honest position is: the software is in development, the Tally XML
          export exists, and everything else on this page is a design demonstration.
        

      


      

        

          Is this a live product I can sign into?+
          

            No. This is the public marketing app running on port 3300. The owner console (5174)
            and mill operations app (4001) are separate development surfaces. Nothing shown in
            the simulator panels is a recording of a real deployment.
          

        


        

          Does the demo form actually send anything?+
          

            It composes your enquiry and downloads it as a text draft on your device. Without a
            configured endpoint, no message leaves your browser — and the form tells you so
            rather than pretending to succeed.
          

        


        

          Which integrations are real today?+
          

            Tally XML export is implemented in the application. Every other connector profile —
            including Tally Prime sync, GST e-invoicing, WhatsApp Business and storefronts — is
            a planned profile. The payload previews are illustrative samples written for this
            page, not documentation of a working API.
          

        


        

          What does Scout actually have access to?+
          

            Scout is intended as a product-question assistant. It is designed to answer questions
            about EverGreen using a Groq-hosted model, with your consent, and it has no access to
            your private business data. In this build there is no configured endpoint, so Scout
            cannot answer — the panel below lets you draft a question instead.
          

        


        

          Are the plans real prices?+
          

            No. They are proposed packages intended to structure a conversation. Commercial
            terms, service levels and support commitments require written confirmation. Treat
            every number in the plans section as indicative.
          

        


        

          Can I run it on my own servers?+
          

            The application is designed to run locally or in a container you control. You supply
            the runtime, database and network. This page explains prerequisites; it does not
            provision any infrastructure for you.
