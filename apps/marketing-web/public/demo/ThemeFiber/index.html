Straight answers

      
Questions worth asking before a demo.

    


    

      

        Is this page connected to a real EverGreen instance?
        
No. This is the public marketing site. It is separate from the owner console and the mill operations app, and it has no access to business records. Simulators, sample payloads and proposed plans here are illustrative.

      


      

        Where do the "Book a demo" and contact forms send my details?
        
Nowhere, unless a delivery endpoint is configured. In this build the form compiles a draft enquiry and downloads it to your device. No transmission occurs and no message is sent to any server.

      


      

        Which integrations actually exist today?
        
The Tally XML export is implemented in the application. The weighbridge, loom PLC, commerce, payments and notification connectors are planned profiles — the payloads shown under Integrations illustrate intended shapes, not working endpoints.

      


      

        Are the plan prices real?
        
No. They are labelled proposed packages for scoping. Commercial terms, service levels and support commitments require written confirmation per deployment.

      


      

        Does the site track me or read my mill data?
        
The marketing app does not access private business data. EverGreen is local-first: data stays on your premise unless a tenant endpoint is deliberately configured at build time.

      


      

        What does the AI assistant have access to?
        
Scout answers product questions from public documentation only, with your consent, and never reads business records. In this build the interface is a preview and is not connected to a live model.
